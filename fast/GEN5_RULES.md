# The gen5 doctrine — apply to EVERY subject from here

Earned the hard way across gen3, gen4 and gen5. Applying these from the start is the whole
point of having written them down.

## Never
- **No outline, rim or edge strokes. Ever.** A boundary is where one side catches light and the
  other does not. In gen3 this ate 45% of the budget and was the single biggest thing all three
  outside critics said made it look fake.
- **No periodic function in a surface normal.** sin(x)+sin(y) as "roughness" prints a diagonal
  grating across the whole frame. Hashed value noise instead.
- **No effect placed on a grid.** A lattice is visible at 1% alpha. Random position, varied
  radius, always.
- **No translucent rect.** A rect in this vocabulary is OPAQUE — the op carries no alpha. Only
  the dab does. Any wash, haze, glaze or veil MUST be dabs.
- **Don't answer "it doesn't look real" with more texture.** The problem is incorrect
  information, not insufficient information.

## Always
- **Flat-gray test first.** The composition must read with zero colour before any colour is
  added. If it fails in gray, no amount of hue will save it.
- **Value plan, then hue as a tint on the value.** Cheapest improvement ever measured: identical
  stroke count, value error down 36%.
- **Light, not object.** Build the thing light does, not the thing itself. A form half-lost in
  shadow is read as correct; that is why chiaroscuro exists.
- **Full lighting model**: direct + sky fill + bounce − occlusion. Bare N·L sends every
  shadowed plane to black. A shadowed face still receives skylight; a crease is dark because it
  is OCCLUDED, which has nothing to do with its normal.
- **Colour temperature carries the light.** Lit runs warm, shadow runs cool from skylight, with
  a bounce from whatever the ground is. One hue family with a brightness ramp reads as tinted,
  never as lit.
- **Structure must be ORGANISED, not scattered.** Randomly oriented planes give shattered glass.
  Terrain is organised by water; a figure by a skeleton; anything else needs its own organiser.
- **Distance loses INFORMATION, not just contrast** — sharpness, saturation, texture grain size,
  shadow depth, and how much structure is resolvable. Haze alone is not depth.
- **The medium itself recedes.** Dab radius grows and opacity falls with distance, or the marks
  give it away.
- **Hierarchy must not become legible AS hierarchy.** Clean nested layers are as fake as no
  layers. Things must intersect, overlap and cut across each other.
- **A wash has two safe regimes and a trap between them.** Many tiny, or few huge. The middle
  clumps. Choose by what it sits on: over a BRIGHT field use many tiny, over dark use few huge.
- **Defects last, ~2%, and barely.** A defect that can be seen as itself has failed.

## Budget, roughly
sky/atmosphere 10 · large masses 20 · light and shadow 25 · distant layers 20 ·
medium structure 15 · fine texture 8 · defects 2 · **outlines 0**
