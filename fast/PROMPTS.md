# The prompts

One short scene description per painting. This is the ONLY input a generation is allowed to
work from. No looking at the previous picture, no porting the previous script — the point of
50 generations is that the same sentence gets painted better, and that only means something if
each generation starts from the sentence.

The original prompts were never written to disk, so these are fixed here as canonical from
gen3 onward. They stay byte-identical for every future generation.

---

**AURORA LAKE** — Aurora over a still lake at night, the light mirrored in the water.

**DAWN RIDGES** — Layered mountain ridges at dawn, receding into haze, with the sun low behind them.

**DEEP LIGHT** — A fish underwater, light falling from the surface above, caustics moving across its body.

**FIRST LIGHT IN THE GROVE** — A redwood grove in fog at first light, with shafts of sun coming through the trees.

**MORNING FIELD** — A horse standing in an open field in early morning light.

**NIGHT RUN** — A car on a wet road at night, its lights broken and stretched in the reflections.

**THE ACORN YEAR** — A big oak tree heavy with acorns, standing alone in a meadow.

**THE GLASS CHOIR** — The inside of a cathedral, light coming through stained glass.

**THE LONG WAIT** — A dog lying on the ground, waiting.

**THE NARROWS** — A slot canyon, light coming down from a narrow opening far above.

**THE WATCHER** — A creature on a branch at night, watching.
